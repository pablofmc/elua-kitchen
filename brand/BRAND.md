Every logo variant comes in two versions: café on a light background, or crema on a dark/café background.

## Typography

No font files or guide in the Drive folder. From the logos:
- **Wordmark "ELÚA"**: high-contrast display face with flared terminals and a swash crossbar on the A. Likely custom or lettered, so always use the logo image, never retype it.
- **Descriptor**: geometric sans (looks like Montserrat). **Unconfirmed.** `logos.ai` and `icono.ai` were checked on 2026-10-16: all text is converted to outlines, so no font names are embedded. `posteos.ai` (44 MB, not downloaded) may still contain live text.
